Designer and manufacturer of high-quality industrial tooling and machinery

Duncan Tool, Inc. is a full-service machine shop, specializing in meeting our customers tooling and machining needs with state of the art CNC machining, design software, and engineering. Located in Tipp City, Ohio, Duncan Tool, serves many industries in Dayton, Ohio and throughout the world. Our services include 3, 4, and 5 axis CNC machining, stamping dies, special machines, fixtures, gages, design and engineering. Duncan Tool, has the experience and know-how to be a valuable partner in the success of your machining, tooling, and equipment projects.
