About Duncan Tool

Duncan Tool, Inc. is a full-service machine shop, specializing in designing and building tooling, machinery, and equipment to suit each customer's specific need and application. Located in Tipp City, Ohio, Duncan Tool, serves many industries in Dayton, Ohio, and throughout the world. Our services include tooling and equipment, custom machining, gages, stamping dies, and assembly. Duncan Tool, has the experience and know-how to be a valuable partner in the success of tooling, machinery and equipment projects.

Who is Duncan Tool?

Duncan Tool, Inc., established in 1992, is a private company, located in Tipp City, Ohio.

In 2013 we expanded our operation to include two adjacent plants, resulting in a total of 11,500 square feet. Duncan Tool, Inc. is located just 5 miles northeast of the intersection of interstate 70 and 75 in southwest Ohio. Duncan Tool, Inc. is centered in the Dayton area and convenient for anyone traveling from the Dayton Airport and surrounding area.

Plant 1

Plant 1 is home to our administrative, sales, and engineering offices, which also includes a large assembly and warehouse.

Plant 2

Plant 2 is our modern, air-conditioned manufacturing facility where we do 2 and 3D machining, plus 3, 4, & 5 Axis CNC machining. Examples of a few machining projects are displayed in the gallery below.
