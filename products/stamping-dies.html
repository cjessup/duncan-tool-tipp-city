Stamping dies

Duncan Tool, Inc. is well known for providing heavy duty, well running and long lasting stamping dies. The difference is in our designs. We utilize proven die-making methods to ensure that our dies perform very well in production. We specialize in "Horn" style dies which utilize a mandrel for supporting cylindrical parts for forming and cutting operations.
