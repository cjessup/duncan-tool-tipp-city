Our equipment

In an industry that demands precision, Duncan Tool, Inc. is fully equipped to meet your needs, with some of the finest equipment and complete and versatile tool rooms. Duncan Tool's departments are managed by a select staff of well-trained CAD Designers, programmers, Toolmakers, apprentices, and Machinists. Duncan Tool works directly with clients, analyzing their requirements, presenting a concept, and providing a complete turn-key system. Duncan Tool prides themselves in their attention to detail, taking system projects from the initial vision, size and material specifications, to an approved conception; followed with its building, production, and then completion, to the final inspection in their quality control department.
