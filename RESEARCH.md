# Duncan Tool, Inc. — research notes

Demo redesign for the GitHub Pages folder. Business facts come from the live site at https://www.duncantool.com/ (home, about, services, contact, employment, and every product page linked from that site). Layout ideas come from peer shops. Nothing below is copied text or branding.

## What the business is

Duncan Tool, Inc. is a full-service machine shop in Tipp City, Ohio. It designs and builds tooling, machinery, and equipment for a customer's application. The live pages name these lines of work:

- 2D and 3D machining, and 3, 4, and 5 axis CNC machining, plus CNC turning, CNC milling, grinding, fabrication, and E.D.M.
- Machining page also lists Wire EDM, jig grinding, water jet and laser cutting, specialty grinding, and large-scale machining
- Stamping dies, including horn-style dies, with stamping listed to 200 tons (die tryout, production, short run)
- Custom machinery and turn-key systems (controls, tooling, framing, hydraulics, pneumatics)
- Gages, tooling, and fixtures, including press systems, weld fixtures, checking fixtures, and inspection gages
- Assembly and finishing
- Technical work: project management, design (SolidWorks 3D, AutoCAD, Master Cam), process documentation, reverse engineering, CMM inspection

Established in 1992. In 2013 the shop expanded to two adjacent plants and 11,500 square feet. Plant 1 is offices, assembly, and warehouse. Plant 2 is the air-conditioned manufacturing floor. Mailing address 4065 Gibson Dr., Tipp City, Ohio 45371. Shipping address 9790 Julie Ct., Tipp City, Ohio 45371. Phone (937) 667-9364. Fax (937) 667-8472. Email dti@duncantool.com. Employment email dave@duncantool.com. Facebook and LinkedIn are linked on the live site. The live footer shows DRMA and Tipp City Area Chamber of Commerce marks; those logos are reused. Hours are not published on the live site and are not shown here.

The original look is a fixed-width table site with a black header, a red-to-black top edge, and a yellow/red/blue oval logo ("The Duncan Tool Difference"). Sampled logo colors: yellow `#F8EF05`, red `#ED1C24`, blue `#0092CD`, black. Link color on the old stylesheet was `#43A0CA`, with hover `#EF4423`. This demo uses the logo yellow, red, and blue on black and warm gray, not a teal or brown template.

## Peer sites

### Manor Tool & Manufacturing — https://manortool.com/

Metal stamping, in-house tool and die, and fabrication in Schiller Park, Illinois. Ideas borrowed:

- A full-width shop photograph with the headline underneath it, not type set on top of the picture
- Each capability links to its own page instead of living only in a sidebar list
- A short path from the customer's starting point to finished work. On this demo that path uses Duncan's own line, from "Part Print" to "Production," on the about page
- A dark closing band with the phone number and a contact button
- A tight row of facts. Only figures Duncan publishes are used: 1992, the 2013 expansion, 11,500 square feet, two plants, and 3, 4, and 5 axis CNC

### Major Tool & Machine — https://www.majortool.com/

Contract machining, fabrication, and assembly in Indianapolis. Ideas borrowed:

- Named machines as a photo grid with the name under the picture (Haas Lathe, Haas Mill, Haas with Rotary, Jig Grinder, Radial Drill, Rotary, Surface Grinder, Water Grinder, CMM, HURCO)
- The two plants described as two separate notes rather than one long paragraph
- Service lists as short labels instead of a column of image bullets
- The same product list Duncan already has, kept as its own pages and gathered in the nav

### Schmid Tool and Engineering — https://www.schmidtool.com/

CNC contract machining, started as a tool and die shop. Ideas borrowed:

- A plain statement of the work, then contact and services actions, without a slogan bar over the photo
- A few supporting lines under that statement, taken from Duncan's home paragraph (CNC machining, design software, engineering)
- Contact repeated after the work sections
- Capabilities as compact chips, not large padded cards

No text, photos, customer lists, tolerances, or certifications were taken from these sites.

## Google reviews

The review block on the home page is **sample**, not real.

Checked:

- The live Duncan Tool site does not show reviews.
- Birdeye (https://reviews.birdeye.com/duncan-tool-148680614497678) lists Duncan Tool at 4065 Gibson Dr. and an aggregate 5.0 from 6 reviews, split on that page as Google (4) and Facebook (2). It does not print any Google review text. The only named item is Katie Greer on Facebook, marked "Recommended," with no written comment. That is not a Google review and is not quoted here.
- Web search did not return a Google Maps or Google Business page that quoted this shop's reviews or a rating that could be verified on Google itself.

Because there was no review text to quote and no verified Google rating, the home section is labeled "Sample reviews — your real Google reviews would appear here." Each card is marked Sample. No star count or review total is stated as fact. The button still links to a Google Maps search for Duncan Tool, Inc. in Tipp City.

Birdeye also shows hours of 7:00 a.m. to 5:30 p.m. every day, including Saturday and Sunday. Those hours are not on duncantool.com. They are not used.
